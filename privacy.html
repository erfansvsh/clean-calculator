Privacy Policy

App: Clean Calculator

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: Clean Calculator is a completely offline utility. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or usage telemetry whatsoever.

1. Information Collection and Use

Clean Calculator does not collect any personal information. You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use the application.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic touch feedback when you tap calculator buttons. It is never used to access device state or collect telemetry.

3. Local Data Storage

Any calculation history or preferences (such as Radian/Degree toggle or sound/haptics toggle) are stored strictly locally on your device's memory. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted across the network.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Clean Calculator does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tracking tools, crash reporters, or tracking libraries.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update our Privacy Policy in the future, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the developer via the official repository or support email.
